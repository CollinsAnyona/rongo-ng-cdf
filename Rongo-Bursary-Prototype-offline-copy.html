Demo steps

  1. On the phone, tap Apply now and sign in with the demo code.
  2. Fill in the guardian details once, then add a child.
  3. Go through the four steps. Tap Add sample photos on the documents step.
  4. Submit. The reference number arrives by SMS.
  5. Switch to CDF office. The new application is at the top, marked New.
  6. Open it. Check the documents, then Request a document or Mark complete.
  7. Record a ward vetting decision with a reason. Open RB-2627-0004 to see a duplicate flag.
  8. In Vetting, approve and lock the list. In Reports, preview the export and audit pack.
  9. Switch back to the phone and tap Track to see the status the parent sees.
